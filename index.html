Mechanism design for
alignment and control

A designer can ask an agent to describe its abilities and tell it what to do. What makes it profitable for the agent to tell the truth, then follow the instruction after it learns something new?

These twelve sheets reconstruct the framework and all five applications of Bergemann, Koh and Morris (2026) through 70 exercises. Each begins with a modeling vignette. Try the problem first, then open its hint or its worked solution.
