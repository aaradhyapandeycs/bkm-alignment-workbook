About the workbook

An agent can hide a tool when it reports its capabilities and still use it after signing a contract. What makes it willing to report honestly and follow an instruction once its private signal arrives?

Work through the paper in 70 exercises across twelve sheets. Each sheet opens with a modeling vignette and includes hints and worked solutions.
